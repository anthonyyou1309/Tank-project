ROADSTERRUSH

An endless thruster run, inspired by the flying Roadster reveal of October 2026

01What the car can do

  • Cold-gas thrustersTen nozzles fed by carbon-wrapped air tanks (COPVs). ↑ thrust-hops over traffic. Every burst uses tank pressure.
  • Self-recompressing tanksNo fuel: the car pumps its own air back in. Pressure refills while your wheels are on a surface. Grab white COPV canisters for a top-up.
  • Mid-air "kick flip"At the reveal it rolled a full 360° over a row of cars. Press ↑ again in the air to kickflip high enough to land on top of a semi.
  • Downward corner jetsThey softened its landing. Hold ↑ to hover, and hard landings are cushioned for you.
  • Side-facing nose jetsThey push the car sideways, so ← → still changes lanes in the air.
  • 2G suction fansThey held the car upside down on a loop ramp. In tunnels, kickflip into the ceiling and the fans lock on. Drive inverted over the barricades, and press ↓ to drop off.
  • 0–60 in under a secondRed Overdrive pickups fire every rear thruster and let you smash straight through obstacles.

03Hazards

  • Barriers & parked cars: hop over them, or land on the roofs
  • Semis: take the ramp, or kickflip up onto them
  • LOW gantries: stay on the road
  • Tunnels: enter on the ground. Dodge barricades below or pipes on the ceiling
